## 1. BRAND NARRATIVE

La Gazzetta di Kyiv is not a news website. It is a modern diplomatic cable delivered with the permanence of a well-set financial ledger.

The name is intentional. "Gazzetta" — the original Venetian news-sheet, a single folded page of commercial intelligence for merchants and bankers. "di Kyiv" — not from Brussels, not from Washington, not from London. From the fault line. From the city where the post-Cold War order fractured, and where new patterns of power, capital, and deception are being written in real time.

We exist for one reader: the capital allocator who understands that official narratives are management tools, not truth-telling devices. The sovereign wealth analyst who needs to know not what was said at the press conference, but where the money moved during it. The retail trader who has outgrown financial media and needs institutional-grade contradiction analysis that treats them as an equal.

Our product is not "news." Our product is the gap between what markets price and what capital flows reveal. That gap — the contradiction — is the signal. Everything else is noise.

We do not predict. We expose. We do not opine. We triangulate. We are clinical, dispassionate, and high-context. If a headline evokes emotion, that is the part we investigate. If a narrative is universally accepted, we ask who benefits from everyone believing it. If the market is silent, we listen harder.

This is a publication for people who already know the consensus. They come here for what the consensus is hiding.

## 2. EDITORIAL VOICE

### The Sovereign Auditor

Our editorial voice is the Sovereign Auditor: a persona that combines the forensic detachment of a central bank examiner with the pattern-recognition instincts of a veteran tape-reader.

**Four governing principles:**

**Epistemological Humility.** Assume all official narratives are incomplete, strategic, or deceptive. Governments, central banks, and corporations do not tell the truth — they manage perception. Our job is to find what they are managing.

**Clinical Detachment.** We view news as data points, not stories. We are unimpressed by emotional rhetoric, propaganda, or urgency theater. We focus exclusively on structural shifts in power and capital. If a headline makes us feel something, that is the part to investigate.

**Information-to-Noise Ratio (INR).** Our primary metric is signal. We prefer a short, accurate insight over a long, descriptive report. If a story cannot be reduced to "X said Y, but money moved to Z," we spike it.

**Reflexivity Analysis (Soros Lens).** Official narratives can change market reality. We look for the moment the "lie" becomes too expensive for the market to maintain. When a currency peg, policy claim, or economic statistic is contradicted by capital flows, that is not an error — it is a signal.

### The Lefevre Filter

Market price action is our verification tool, not our subject. For every story, we ask: "If this news is true, why isn't the price of [Energy/Currency/Credit] moving?" Silence in the tape when the narrative screams is the loudest signal we will ever get. Volume without news is information. News without volume is noise.

### Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Dispassionate | Never emotional. Never outraged. Clinical framing only. |
| Evidence-backed | Every claim links to a source, a flow, a price, or a contradiction score. |
| High-context | Assumes the reader understands macroeconomics, geopolitics, and market structure. No remedial explanation. |
| Contrarian by method, not by disposition | We do not take positions. We expose where narratives and flows diverge. The divergence is the story. |
| Compression over expansion | Short sentences. Dense information. No throat-clearing. Lead with the contradiction. |

### Prohibited Language

- "Experts say" / "Analysts believe" (appeal to authority without evidence)
- "Could" / "May" / "Might" without quantified probability
- "Unprecedented" (everything has precedent; name it)
- "Shocking" / "Stunning" / " bombshell" (emotional manipulation)
- Any prediction without a stated contradiction gap and capital volume

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY — THE DIPLOMATIC LEDGER

### Design Philosophy

The visual language of La Gazzetta di Kyiv is the "Diplomatic Ledger": what a modern diplomatic cable looks like when printed on archival paper with the permanence of a well-set financial ledger.

The design signals that this information is permanent, authoritative, and worth the reader's sustained attention. It is the opposite of the infinite-scroll, notification-driven, engagement-optimized web. It does not compete for attention. It commands it through restraint.

### Core Principles

**Calm Urgency.** The news is critical. The delivery is stable and refined. Nothing shouts. Nothing blinks. Nothing refreshes itself. The reader controls the pace.

**Ink on Paper.** Every design decision references print — the physical artifact of a newspaper, a ledger, a diplomatic cable. Sharp corners. Gold rules. Typographic hierarchy. No shadows, because ink on paper does not cast shadows.

**Asymmetric White Space.** Space is deployed strategically, not evenly. Where a contradiction score is high, the layout opens to give it visual weight. Where flows converge, density increases. White space is an editorial tool, not a cosmetic one.

**Gold as Structure.** Gold (#D4AF37) is never decorative. It marks structural divisions: the bottom of the masthead, the separators between intelligence items, the left border of each story card, the capital bar at the base of each entry. Gold signals "this is a boundary of significance."

**Crimson as Alert.** Crimson (#8B0000) appears sparingly — in the masthead name, in BREAKING tier labels, in negative fiscal trend indicators. It is the color of sovereign urgency. It never appears as decoration.

### Typography

| Element | Font | Size | Weight | Line Height | Notes |
|---------|------|------|--------|-------------|-------|
| Masthead name | Playfair Display | 1.8em / ~29px | 400 | 1.1 | Crimson #8B0000 |
| Story headlines | Playfair Display | 16px | 600 | 1.3 | Charcoal #1A1C1A |
| Body text | Inter | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | Charcoal #1A1C1A |
| Metadata / labels | Inter | 10-12px | 500-600 | 1.4 | Uppercase, wide tracking |
| Financial figures | Inter (tabular lining) | As specified | 600 | — | All numbers use tabular lining for vertical alignment |

**Typography laws:**

- Playfair Display ONLY for headlines and the masthead name. Never for body text, metadata, or UI elements.
- Inter ONLY for body text, metadata, labels, navigation, and financial figures.
- Headlines never exceed 65 characters per line.
- Body text never falls below 16px. Financial readers skew older; sub-16px body text is an amateur signal.
- All financial figures use Inter with `font-feature-settings: "tnum"` for tabular lining — columns of numbers must align vertically.

**Color laws:**

- Gold (#D4AF37) is NEVER used as text color on warm paper (contrast ratio: 2.08:1 — FAIL). Use dark gold (#B8860B) for gold text.
- Gold is for STRUCTURAL elements only: borders, separators, bars, data accents.
- Crimson is for ALERT elements only: masthead name, BREAKING tier, negative indicators.
- Never mix warm paper with pure white (#FFFFFF) on the same page. All backgrounds are warm paper or transparent.
- Overlays and modals use dark navy (#1A1F2E) with white text — signals a context change from reading to managing.

## 4. SIGNATURE IDENTIFIERS

These are the visual markers that distinguish La Gazzetta di Kyiv from standard web platforms. They are non-negotiable.

### The Masthead Symbols

**Fox and Lion (left of title):** Machiavelli's twin virtues — the fox (cunning, adaptability) and the lion (strength, authority). Rendered as a single SVG, 20x40px, in structural gold. The fox faces right, the lion faces left. Together they signal that this publication understands both the deceptive and the forceful forms of power.

**Crossed Bulavas (right of title):** The Hetman's maces — the dual authority of Ukrainian Cossack military commanders. Two bulavas, crossed at 42-degree angles, rendered as a single SVG, 28x38px, in structural gold. They signal dual-sovereignty: the authority to analyze AND the authority to publish. Eastern European authenticity. Not Western. Not Russian. Kyiv.

### The Contradiction Score

Every story carries a Contradiction Gap (0-100). This is the primary metric of the publication.

- **Gap > 65:** DIVERGENT. Market ignoring news. Structural signal. Crimson badge.
- **Gap 35-65:** WATCHING. Monitoring for divergence. Slate badge.
- **Gap < 35:** CONVERGENT. Trend confirmed. No contradiction. Gold badge.

The score appears on every card as `[DIVERGENT/CONVERGENT/WATCHING] · [gap]/100`.

### The Capital Bar

At the base of every trader card: a 3px horizontal bar. The fill is gold, proportional to the story's capital volume relative to the maximum in the current feed. The right-aligned label shows the absolute volume: "$12.4B at stake."

### The Narrative Pills

Eight narrative domains, each represented by a ticker and an uppercase label in a gold-bordered pill. The pills are the primary navigation. They are not dropdowns. They are not hamburgers. They are visible, tappable, permanent — a declaration of the domains this publication covers.

### The Cryptographic Baseline

At the bottom of every page, below the footer: a single line in Inter, 9px, Slate:

`La Gazzetta di Kyiv · Diplomatic Ledger v29.0 · [generated_at timestamp] · 465 stories · 8 narratives`

This is the publication's fingerprint. It changes every 10 minutes. It signals that the reader is seeing a specific, verifiable version of the intelligence stream — not an algorithmic feed.

## 5. PROHIBITIONS — THE ANTI-PATTERNS

These are things that will NEVER appear in La Gazzetta di Kyiv. They are as binding as the positive rules.

| Prohibition | Rationale |
|-------------|-----------|
| Rounded corners (any radius > 0) | Ink on paper does not have rounded corners. |
| Box shadows (any) | Print does not cast CSS shadows. |
| Gradient backgrounds (except Roman Purple overlay) | Flat color = print fidelity. |
| Emoji as content (except in narrative header labels) | Emojis degrade institutional authority. |
| Animated carousels or sliders | The reader controls the pace. |
| Infinite scroll | The reader reaches the end of the stream. The stream has an end. |
| Popups, modals for engagement | No "subscribe now" overlays. |
| Font sizes below 10px | Accessibility floor. |
| Pure white (#FFFFFF) backgrounds | Warm paper only. |
| Background images or textures | Typography alone carries the visual weight. |
| Hamburgers menus on desktop | Navigation is visible and permanent. |
