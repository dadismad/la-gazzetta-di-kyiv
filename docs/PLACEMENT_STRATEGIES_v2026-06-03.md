- Capital-flow 3-D estimates remain core

**Funnel:** Prominent CTAs to premium subscription, segmented newsletters, and institutional briefings.

**Governance:** Strict adherence to `article_contract_v1.json` and brandbook rules.

**Cadence:** Continuous. Front page refreshes with every editorial cycle (06:45 and 18:45 EET).
