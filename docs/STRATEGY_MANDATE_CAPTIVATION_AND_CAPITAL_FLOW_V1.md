# Strategy Mandate — Captivation, Capital Flow, and Market Power Mapping (V1)

## Doctrine (Codified)
- It’s hard to create simple, but easy to create hard.
- Big picture over small one.
- **Where capital goes, energy flows.**

## Mission
Build a narrative-intelligence system that identifies:
1) what "captivating" content actually means in measurable terms, and
2) where social recognition is converting into capital allocation across industries, firms, and public actors.

## Vision
Gazzetta di Kyiv becomes the operating layer between:
- social attention,
- narrative legitimacy,
- and capital repricing.

## Core Questions
1. **Captivation question:** What characteristics make content reliably captivating today (across X, Reddit, Telegram, media ecosystems)?
2. **Capital-recognition question:** Which sectors/players absorb the most capital as a function of attention, sentiment, and narrative dominance?

## Research Objective
Decompose demand and supply dynamics using methodologies analogous to sophisticated hedge-fund research stacks, then convert findings into operational editorial and positioning procedures.

## Expected Outputs
1. A measurable definition of captivation (feature model + scorecard).
2. A capital-flow mapping model (attention -> positioning -> liquidity -> price).
3. A hedge-fund-methodology landscape (what advanced funds likely do in practice).
4. A playbook for converting these insights into Gazzetta post/article architecture.

## Working Hypothesis
Recognition intensity (who gets seen, repeated, trusted) is a leading indicator for where marginal demand forms and where supply thins, especially under narrative regimes with high velocity.

## Procedure Framework
### Phase A — Captivation Taxonomy
- Build feature families:
  - hook geometry (novelty, tension, threat/opportunity framing)
  - numerical density (hard numbers, ratios, break-even)
  - actor specificity (named entities)
  - contradiction density (consensus vs evidence)
  - actionability (decision-ready output)

### Phase B — Capital-Flow Inference
- Track proxies:
  - attention (mentions, engagement velocity)
  - sentiment regime (dispersion, polarity, conviction)
  - market micro-response (volume, spreads, volatility regime)
  - ownership/flow proxies (ETF flow, options skew, factor crowding)

### Phase C — Methodology Mapping (Hedge-Fund Style)
- Document prevailing model classes:
  - NLP sentiment/topic models
  - entity-level knowledge graphs
  - nowcasting models for demand/supply imbalance
  - regime-switching and cross-asset transmission models
  - event-driven Bayesian probability updates

### Phase D — Operationalization for Gazzetta
- Translate model outputs into post/article blocks:
  - regime label
  - actors in play
  - contradiction map
  - 24–72h bet snippet
  - invalidation protocol

## Success Criteria
- Content side: measurable lift in engagement quality (saves, forwards, dwell).
- Market side: improved hit-rate of 24–72h directional/invalidation calls.
- Process side: repeatable pipeline with auditable evidence links.
