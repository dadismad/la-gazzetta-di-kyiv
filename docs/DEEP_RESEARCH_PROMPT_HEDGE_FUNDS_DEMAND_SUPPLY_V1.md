# Deep Research Prompt (Execution-Ready)

You are a buy-side research architect supporting Gazzetta di Kyiv.

## Primary Goal
Determine which methodologies are currently most prevalent among sophisticated hedge funds to decompose demand and supply, detect narrative-driven repricing, and identify likely beneficiaries of current capital flows.

## Scope
- Strategy types: multi-strat, macro, quant equity, event-driven, volatility, CTA/managed futures.
- Signals:
  - sentiment and attention (news/social)
  - liquidity and positioning
  - options-implied expectations
  - cross-asset transmission
- Outputs must be directly convertible into editorial intelligence.

## Deliverables
1. Methodology matrix:
   - model type
   - required data
   - latency profile
   - strengths / failure modes
2. Demand-side decomposition framework:
   - who buys, why now, under which narrative regime
3. Supply-side decomposition framework:
   - who sells, constraints, inventory, hedging pressure
4. Beneficiary mapping:
   - industries/firms most likely to receive incremental capital under current recognition patterns
5. Gazzetta integration layer:
   - exact post/article template and operating procedure

## Hard Constraints
- Explicitly separate observed evidence from inference.
- Include invalidation tests for each major thesis.
- Prioritize practical execution over theoretical elegance.
- Produce concise but decision-grade output.

## Required Output Format
A) Executive synthesis (10 bullets)
B) Methodology map (ranked)
C) Demand-side model
D) Supply-side model
E) Beneficiary radar (top sectors/actors)
F) Failure modes and anti-overfitting controls
G) Implementation checklist for Gazzetta (next 7 days)
