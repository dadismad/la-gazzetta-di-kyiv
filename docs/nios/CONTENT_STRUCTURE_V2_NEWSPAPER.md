# Content Structure V2 — Newspaper + NI Lens

## Daily Mix
- 1 Lead story (macro/politics/geopolitics)
- 3–6 Supporting stories
- 1 NI Column (interpretation)

## Section Intent
- Macro: policy, liquidity, inflation, rates, growth
- Politics: electoral incentives, governance risks, policy feasibility
- Geopolitics: power shifts, trade routes, sanctions, security risks
- NI Column: actor-incentive-contradiction lens

## Output Formats
- Website: full structured stories
- Telegram: compact alert summaries
- Reddit: long-form discussion and hypothesis testing

## Quality Gate
Every published story must include:
- factual lead,
- why-now relevance,
- at least one invalidation condition,
- no fabricated allegations.
