# Gazzetta di Kyiv — Brand Book (v1)

## Vision
Make global macro-financial narratives understandable and actionable for retail traders.

## Mission
Deliver narrative-first intelligence with disciplined risk framing, transparent uncertainty, and consistent editorial identity across all products.

## Core Principles
1. Narrative clarity over quant jargon.
2. Actionability with explicit invalidation conditions.
3. Consistency across surfaces (web, briefs, bots, social).
4. Trust through auditability and source attribution.

## Voice & Tone
- Clear, literate, concise.
- Professional, calm, non-hype.
- No repetitive phrasing across modules.

## Visual System
- Palette (strict): #F7FAFF, #3E6FAE, #10233F, #6BB6FF
- Typography: compact operational UI scale (8–10 body, 10 headings on command surfaces)
- Layout: line-based, low-friction, minimal framing, high scanability.

## Content System
Each narrative must include:
1) What is happening
2) Why now
3) 3-day capital flow estimate
4) 3-day asset projection
5) Action plan + invalidation trigger

## Product Scope
Applies to:
- Website home and focus panels
- Data Desk summaries
- Daily/weekly briefs
- Automation outputs and social snippets

## Governance
- Brand gate required before publish.
- Violations block deploy and generate remediation tasks.
