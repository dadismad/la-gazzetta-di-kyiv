# Gazzetta di Kyiv — Mission, Vision, Goals, Priorities, Objectives

## Mission
- Deliver short-to-medium term, investment-oriented reviews of economic, political, and geopolitical developments through representative narratives from newspapers and social media.
- Serve mature, socially engaged, market-aware audiences (professionals, business owners, investors, advocates, technocrats) with concise, decision-relevant intelligence.
- Provide perspective informed by decades of exposure to developed-world institutions and sophisticated corporate circles.

## Vision
- In an always-on world of noisy signals, preparedness is alpha.
- Perspective is the king; disciplined interpretation is the castle.
- Convert narrative turbulence into tactical clarity and actionable positioning signals.

## Operating Goals (90 days)
1. Build an always-on narrative intelligence engine with auditable ingestion.
2. Rank and refresh source universe daily for tactical relevance.
3. Publish daily narrative briefs and weekly regime maps.
4. Maintain measurable quality: coverage breadth, freshness, precision, explainability.

## Priorities
1. Source quality and accessibility (API/RSS-first, scraping fallback)
2. Narrative extraction quality (topic, sentiment, velocity, cross-source confirmation)
3. Operational reliability (3x/day publication minimum; failure alerts)
4. Product presentation (dense numerical review + clear hooks)

## Objectives & KPIs
- Ingestion freshness: >=95% successful scheduled runs
- Coverage: >=40 high-signal sources across politics/econ/geopolitics/markets/tech
- Narrative output: daily top 10 narratives with score + confidence + trend delta
- Latency: website refresh within 15 minutes after scheduled ingest
- Auditability: every published insight traceable to source URLs and timestamps

## Governance
- Attribute every claim to source links.
- Distinguish observed signal vs inference.
- Preserve uncertainty indicators and confidence scores.
- No credential exposure in outputs/logs.
