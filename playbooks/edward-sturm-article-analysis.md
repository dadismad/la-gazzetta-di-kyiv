# Edward Sturm Article Analysis — Marketing Tactics for Gazzetta Playbook

## Article 1: Landing Page Conversion Checklist
**URL:** https://edwardsturm.com/articles/landing-page-conversion-checklist/
**Published:** June 1, 2026

### Core Thesis
You can 25x revenue without any extra traffic simply by fixing landing page conversion — most businesses under-convert the visitors they already have.

### Actionable Tactics / Checklist Items
1. **Hook in the first viewport (above the fold)** — 60% of people never scroll. Use simple, outcome-oriented language. Mantra: *clarity beats cleverness*. State exactly what the product/service is and the main benefit immediately.
2. **Make trust obvious immediately** — best reviews at the top, video testimonials with outcomes, customer count, years in business, client logos, certifications, press mentions, guarantees. Don't hide social proof.
3. **Kill "false bottoms"** — add a down arrow showing a preview of the next section so visitors know there's more below.
4. **List outcomes not just features** — instead of listing deliverables, list expected results ("Save 5-10+ hours per month", "Make faster decisions"). Bullets, short sentences, short paragraphs.
5. **Add a phone number** — puts a real business behind the site. Most people won't call, but it builds massive trust. Use a shortcode so you can swap the number centrally if it gets spammed.
6. **Explain why you ask for data** — "Email Address (required for order confirmation)" converts better than bare form fields.
7. **Write with "You", not "the customer"** — speak directly to the reader.
8. **Buyer-intent FAQs** — answer objections people have right before purchase ("What if it doesn't work?", "Is there a contract?"). Don't use accordions on SEO pages.
9. **Use media so clear visitors barely need to read** — show product, result, or transformation. No stock images.

### Metrics / Results Claimed
- $1,000/month → $25,000/month from one landing page redesign (25x)
- "Dramatic increase in sales started happening immediately"

### Gazzetta Takeaway
Gazzetta's landing pages (donation/subscribe pages) should test the Sturm approach: outcome-driven headlines above the fold, trust signals (press mentions, partner logos), a phone number/human touch, and "explain why" form fields.

## Article 2: Google I/O — Biggest Search Changes in 25 Years
**URL:** https://edwardsturm.com/articles/google-io-biggest-search-changes-get-more-business/
**Published:** May 24, 2026

### Core Thesis
Google's AI-powered search changes (hyper-long prompts, SERP mini-apps, AI agents) are the biggest in 25 years, but the way to win is simple: focus on transactional intent, social discovery, customer service, and link-building — not panic.

### Actionable Tactics / Checklist Items
1. **If you're a publisher, sell something** — AdSense isn't reliable, affiliate links get stripped by AI. Become a business with an SEO-informed media arm, not just a content play.
2. **Use overlooked social platforms for SEO** — YouTube, Instagram, Reddit, LinkedIn are some of the most-clicked sites in search. Start by posting all reviews transcribed as video with opening lines "brand name + review".
3. **Target decision-stage searchers** — people looking to buy, call, or use something. AI won't re-tile your roof. Figure out the language people use when ready to take action.
4. **Make competitor comparison pages** — AI loves comparison tables. Create "Alternative to [Competitor]" pages with a you-vs-them table.
5. **Max out on customer service** — personalized experience > anonymous brand. Happy customers = fewer bad reviews on Reddit/social.
6. **Get websites linking to yours with brand info** — authoritative backlinks make you appear in more searches and inform AI who you are.
7. **Get into frequently-cited listicles** — email the author and ask what it takes to be included. Lead with money.
8. **Build a dedicated reviews hub** — buy "BrandName + Reviews + .com". AI searches reviews of your brand there.
9. **Constantly share company updates** — new offerings, awards, nominations. Keep a stream of fresh signals.

### Top 4 to Focus On
Sturm's priority ranking: (8) customer service, (3) decision-stage targeting, (2) social media discoverability, (6) link-building.

### Metrics / Results Claimed
- "Changes will take years or decades" — long rollout window
- Most people won't use long prompts or wait for AI agents (defender's advantage)

### Gazzetta Takeaway
Gazzetta should: (a) remain a business-first media operation (already selling something — it's the news/credibility), (b) carpet-bomb decision-stage searches related to Ukraine/Russia news and analysis, (c) get linked from authoritative listicles and news roundups, (d) build a reviews/testimonials hub for the brand.

## Article 3: Review Websites Summarizing Review Websites — The New AI Reputation Playbook (Corrected URL)
**URL:** https://edwardsturm.com/articles/review-websites-summarizing-review-websites-ai-seo-reputation-playbook/
**Published:** May 18, 2026

### Core Thesis
AI is now regurgitating fake/malicious reviews of brands (including AI-generated YouTube channels with fake avatars), so you must proactively flood the zone with real reviews in the formats AI reads — dedicated review sites, video transcripts, and social media — to drown out the fakes.

### Actionable Tactics / Checklist Items
1. **Share reviews everywhere with searchable formatting** — video reviews to YouTube/Instagram/Facebook/X/LinkedIn. Written reviews screenshot and shared. ALL reviews transcribed with posts opening "brand name + review" (what people and LLMs search for).
2. **Buy "BrandNameReviews.com"** — a dedicated review repository site. Full transcripts of video testimonials, written testimonials, customer detail (with permission). Branded as an extension of your brand, not a third-party site. Link to it from your main site for link juice.
3. **Build a tier-2 summary site (inception strategy)** — "BrandNameReviewsSummary.com" or "BrandNameReviewsReport.com". AI-summarized overview of the primary review site for people who don't want to read a long page. Again branded. "Summarizing reviews from [PrimarySite] for people who want a concise overview."
4. **Attach a Facebook pixel to the review sites** — then run retargeting ads featuring testimonials. Everyone who visits these sites sees positive reviews in their Facebook/Instagram feed.
5. **Call out fake claims about your brand** — untested but LLMs have no memory of reviews; responses change constantly.

### Metrics / Results Claimed
- "AI generated YouTube channels… with reviews made WITHOUT BUYING THE PRODUCT" — this is actively happening
- ChatGPT cites fake YouTube review videos because it can't distinguish real from fake

### Gazzetta Takeaway
Gazzetta should build a dedicated reviews/testimonials microsite (gazzettareviews.com or similar), share reader testimonials everywhere with transcribed text opening with "Gazzetta di Kyiv review", and consider a summary page. Crucially — attach a pixel and retarget. This protects against bad-faith review attacks in the Ukraine information space.

## Article 4: The SEO Colonies Strategy — Turn One Ranking Page Into Dozens
**URL:** https://edwardsturm.com/articles/seo-colonies-strategy-turn-one-page-into-dozens/
**Published:** April 19, 2026

### Core Thesis
Ranking pages generate their own SEO authority (from satisfied clicks, not just backlinks); you can pass this authority to new pages via internal links like a real-time strategy game sending troops to new colonies, compounding your organic footprint exponentially.

### Actionable Tactics / Checklist Items
1. **The colony mechanic** — Page A ranks and gets satisfied clicks (people don't pogo-stick back to search). Link Page A → Page B (uncompetitive target keyword). Page A passes authority to Page B. Page B starts ranking.
2. **Troop rotation** — Once Page B is stable at rank, remove Page A's link and re-deploy it to Page C. Don't let any page become orphaned — always keep at least one internal link to every page.
3. **Start with the homepage** — Rank your homepage for your brand name first (easiest thing in SEO). Build backlinks to it. Then link homepage to 2-3 pages targeting uncompetitive keywords (initial colonies).
4. **Target easy keywords = colonies** — Two types: (a) People Also Ask questions (low competition, top-of-funnel awareness), (b) Bottom-of-funnel keywords (high intent, lower search volume → fewer competitors targeting them).
5. **Compounding strategy** — Use ranking colonies to rank for more easy keywords. Build enough authority to eventually target competitive keywords. Scale strategically.

### Metrics / Results Claimed
- "These pages drove an extra $3 million in revenue from organic search over the following year" — Dominick DeJoy
- "Booked about $100,000 of business through SEO in my first year" — Robert Brill
- "Ranking #1 on Google for 25+ keywords and hundreds on Bing — with a new site — after two months" — Jacob Darrah
- "$4,000 sale within the first six weeks" — Omar Abu-Shaaban

### Gazzetta Takeaway
Gazzetta should identify its top-10 ranking pages (its "colonies") and systematically link them to new, uncompetitive keyword pages — especially bottom-of-funnel queries like "Ukraine war analysis", "Russia sanctions list 2026", "Kyiv news today" — that readers search with purchase/subscribe intent. Rotate internal links monthly from established colonies to new targets.

## Cross-Article Synthesis: Recurring Themes for Gazzetta

1. **AI-optimized content formatting** — Write for LLMs as much as humans. Use clear transcripts, comparison tables, structured data, and "brand name + keyword" openings.
2. **Reviews / social proof everywhere** — Multiple articles converge on this: reviews on your site, a dedicated reviews hub, social media with transcriptions, retargeting ads.
3. **Decision-stage targeting > informational** — Focus on people ready to act (subscribe, donate, share) rather than casual browsers.
4. **Internal link authority farming (colonies)** — Use ranking pages as engines to bootstrap new pages.
5. **Trust signals are conversion infrastructure** — Phone numbers, press logos, guarantees, video testimonials — these aren't optional.
6. **Sell something** — Don't be pure media; be a business with a media arm. Gazzetta is well positioned here (news as the product, donations/subscriptions as revenue).
