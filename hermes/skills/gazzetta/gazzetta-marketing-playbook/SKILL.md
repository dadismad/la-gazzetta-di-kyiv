# Gazzetta Marketing Playbook — Edward Sturm Analysis

8 articles analyzed (May–June 2026). Tactics organized by category with Gazzetta-specific applications.

## 1. Landing Page Conversion (25x Revenue Without Extra Traffic)

**Source:** [Landing Page Conversion Checklist](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/landing-page-conversion-checklist/)

**Tactics:**
- Hook above the fold with outcome language — *clarity beats cleverness.* 60% never scroll.
- Trust signals everywhere: best reviews at top, video testimonials, customer count, years active, press logos, certifications, phone number
- Kill "false bottoms" — down arrow previews next section
- List outcomes, not features — "Save 5-10h/month" > "Weekly reports"
- Buyer-intent FAQs at bottom (not accordions for SEO pages)
- Media so clear visitors barely need to read — show product/result, no stock images

**Gazzetta action:** Gazzetta's landing/subscribe/donate pages: outcome-driven H1, trust bar with press mentions, "explain why" on form fields.

## 2. Google's Biggest Search Changes in 25 Years (AI Search)

**Source:** [Google I/O Search Changes](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/google-io-biggest-search-changes-get-more-business/)

**Tactics:**
- **Priority #1:** Customer service (#8 on Sturm's list). Happy customers = fewer bad reviews on Reddit/social.
- **Priority #2:** Target decision-stage searchers — people ready to buy/call/subscribe. AI won't re-tile your roof.
- **Priority #3:** Social media discoverability — YouTube, Instagram, Reddit, LinkedIn are most-clicked sites in search
- Build competitor comparison pages — AI loves tables
- Get into frequently-cited listicles — email authors, lead with money
- Build dedicated reviews hub — buy "BrandNameReviews.com"

**Gazzetta action:** Focus on decision-stage queries ("Ukraine analysis today", "Russia sanctions update"), carpet-bomb with video transcripts, build review hub.

## 3. AI Reputation Defense (Review Website Inception)

**Source:** [AI Reputation Playbook](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/review-websites-summarizing-review-websites-ai-seo-reputation-playbook/)

**Tactics:**
- Flood zone with real reviews in AI-readable formats: video transcripts, dedicated sites, social posts
- Buy "BrandNameReviews.com" — dedicated review repository with full transcripts
- **Inception strategy:** Build a tier-2 summary site ("BrandNameReviewsSummary.com") — AI-summarized overview for people who don't read long pages
- Attach Facebook pixel → retarget visitors with testimonial ads
- Multi-platform defense: YouTube + Reels + TikTok + LinkedIn + X + Threads

**Gazzetta action:** Build gazzettareviews.com with reader/analyst testimonials, video transcripts, pixel retargeting. Summary page for AI crawling.

## 4. SEO Colonies Strategy (Compound Organic Growth)

**Source:** [SEO Colonies](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/seo-colonies-strategy-turn-one-page-into-dozens/)

**Tactics:**
- Ranking pages generate their own authority from satisfied clicks — pass it via internal links to new pages
- **Colony mechanic:** Page A (ranking) → link to Page B (uncompetitive target) → B starts ranking → rotate link to Page C
- Target two types: (a) People Also Ask questions (low comp, awareness), (b) bottom-of-funnel keywords (high intent, low volume)
- Start with homepage for brand name → link to 2-3 easy targets → compound
- Never orphan a page — at least one internal link always

**Results cited:** $3M extra revenue from organic (DeJoy), 25+ #1 rankings in 2 months (Darrah), $100K first-year SEO bookings (Brill)

**Gazzetta action:** Identify top-10 ranking Gazzetta pages → systematically link to new pages targeting "Ukraine war analysis", "Kyiv news today", "Russia sanctions 2026" — rotate monthly.

## 5. Viral Video — 5x Sales From 20 Minutes of Work

**Source:** [Viral Video Marketing](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/viral-video-marketing-5x-sales-20-minutes-of-work/)

**Tactics:**
- Post daily without perfectionism — 20-min midnight video did 5.2M views
- Hijack attention with recognizable brands/icons (Airbnb, Nike, Gmail) — outperform generic visuals
- Wear/display product branding in every video — "gentle reminder" for consideration-stage viewers
- Share testimonials relentlessly — the second version of the same video sold 5x more because months of review-sharing built trust
- Iterate on proven evergreen content — move best stat to the hook, add bigger brand names
- Expand surface area for luck — daily marketing = more unpredictable wins

**Metrics:** 5.2M views, +15,500 followers, 5x sales day, +1,115% social traffic

**Gazzetta action:** Post daily Gazzetta-branded content even when imperfect. Recycle evergreen stories with better hooks. Share reader testimonials as standalone content.

## 6. 15-Minute "No Thinking" Video System

**Source:** [15-Min Video System](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/15-minute-no-thinking-video-system-500k-views/)

**Tactics:**
- Phone back-camera (4K) → AirDrop → Descript AI editing
- Descript Underlord: "Shorten word gaps >0.25s to 0s" + "Remove retakes"
- Edit via transcript (not timeline) — changing text changes video
- Saved caption style layer for brand consistency
- **Description strategy:** one keyword-rich sentence + entire video transcript → long-tail SEO
- Export 4K always, even phone-shot verticals

**Metrics:** 1,274 consecutive daily videos (since Nov 2022), multiple 500K-view hits from 15-min productions

**Gazzetta action:** Build dead-simple pipeline: phone → AI edit → captions → transcript-as-description. Accept "decent enough" quality.

## 7. Video SEO Discoverability System

**Source:** [Video SEO System](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/video-seo-content-discoverability-system/)

**Tactics:**
- **Two-prompt ChatGPT system:** (1) "Here's the transcript. Give me a good title." (2) "Write YouTube description. Detailed. Bullets. No emojis. No buzzwords."
- Proofread, add personal style, ensure target keyword in first sentence
- Mobile shorts: corrected transcript = description, first line of transcript = title
- Video filenames: always named with target keyword (appear in Google Images)
- Works for entire funnel: top (discoverability) → middle (comparison) → bottom (purchase)
- **Niche advantage:** Most niches have zero SEO-savvy video creators. Following this in a less competitive niche = superpower.

**Gazzetta action:** Every Gazzetta video: ChatGPT title + description → proofread → full transcript in description → keyword in filename. Dominate search for Ukraine war/news analysis queries.

## 8. Fake AI Review Hijacking — Brand Defense

**Source:** [AI Review Hijacking](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/fake-ai-review-videos-hijacking-brand-google-chatgpt/)

**Tactics:**
- **Defensive:** Compile video testimonials, read written reviews aloud on video, explain how you overcome objections, refer to brand by name (not "us")
- **Offensive:** Make honest competitor comparison videos — title format: "[Competitor] Review: Is It Legit?" — after actually trying their product
- **Multi-platform:** YouTube + Instagram Reels + Facebook + TikTok + LinkedIn + X + Threads
- Release 1-3x/month (not all at once) for ranking signals
- Target non-branded purchase-intent keywords on both website AND video

**Key data:** YouTube = #1 most-clicked site in Google + #1 most-cited site in AI search. ChatGPT cites fake review videos because it can't distinguish real from fake.

**Gazzetta action:** Create honest "review" videos comparing Gazzetta's Ukraine coverage vs BBC/Reuters/Al Jazeera. Flood search for "best Ukraine war coverage" with Gazzetta content. Compile reader testimonials into multi-platform video.

## Cross-Cutting Principles

| Principle | Application |
|---|---|
| **Consistency > Perfection** | Post daily, even imperfect. 1,274 consecutive days → compound. |
| **Transcripts = SEO fuel** | Full video transcripts in descriptions = massive long-tail keyword surface |
| **ChatGPT as editor, not author** | Use for drafts, always proofread, remove AI giveaways |
| **Brand everywhere** | Name in every description, filename with keywords, wear/display in video |
| **Reviews are content assets** | Testimonials rank, get cited by AI, build trust over months |
| **Multi-platform saturation** | Same content → different descriptions per platform → occupy multiple SERP positions |
| **Familiarity hijacks attention** | Recognizable brands/icons in thumbnails stop scroll and rank |
| **Surface area for luck** | Daily output = more chances for the unpredictable viral hit |
| **Sell something** | Be a business with a media arm, not pure media. Gazzetta positioned well here. |
