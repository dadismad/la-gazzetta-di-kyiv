## Principles

1. **Never literal**: "Track Record" ≠ "Послужной список" (sounds like an HR file). Use "Трек-Рекорд" — it's a recognized term in Russian finance.
2. **Keep brand terms**: "INTEL", "ALPHA", "C-SUITE" are branding, not descriptive text. Keep in English.
3. **Trading-desk tone**: Use terms a Russian-speaking trader on a Telegram channel would recognize — "телеметрия", "альфа", "трек-рекорд" are standard.
4. **Short for nav**: Navigation labels must fit in 1-2 words. "Интел-Репорты" not "Интеллектуальные Репорты".
5. **Verify Cyrillic**: Always check output contains Cyrillic characters. DeepSeek sometimes outputs Chinese (CJK) — see pitfall in parent skill.
