## Future Design Direction: Nebula Grid (Option B)

User requested "space constellation form" visualization. Three options were
evaluated June 2026. Recommendation: **Option B — Nebula Grid** with
Option A constellation elements.

### Option B Specification

- 8 narrative sections arranged in CSS Grid (2×4 on desktop, 1-column mobile)
- Each section has faint gold radial gradient ("nebula" background)
- Story cards within each nebula sized proportionally to capital flow volume
- Large cards = $50B+ flows. Small chips = sub-$1B flows
- Cards sorted by capital volume within each narrative
- Information Warfare: gold badge + split-dot on contradictory stories
- Filter bar: "Sort by: Capital Volume | Contradiction | Freshness"
- Toggle: "Show internal contradictions only"

### Option A Elements to Incorporate

- Mini SVG constellation in masthead showing 8 narrative stars connected
- Click any star → scrolls to that narrative section
- Serves as navigation + brand mark without dark palette shift
