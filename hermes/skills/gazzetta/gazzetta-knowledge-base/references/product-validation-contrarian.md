# Product Validation for Contrarian Platforms

**Principle:** Focus groups designed to find consensus CANNOT validate a contrarian product edge.
Asking average consumers what they want produces requests for what already exists — the exact "academic trap" that Gazzetta's architecture rejects.

**User directive (June 23, 2026):** "Kill the focus group idea immediately. If you ask a focus group of average consumers or casual retail traders what they want, they will ask for exactly what already exists. Your value proposition is inherently contrarian. Focus groups are designed to find consensus. You cannot use a consensus-seeking tool to validate a contrarian edge."

## Correct Validation Methods

1. **Telegram Forward Rates** — In the attention economy, a forward is proof of value. If Tier 3 Macro Lens gets forwarded to other trading groups, the value prop is validated.

2. **10-Trader Syndicate** — Hand-selected group of active market participants. If features are useless, they'll ignore them. If usability is bad, they'll tell you exactly where it breaks.

3. **Paper-Trading PnL** — The market is the focus group. If the system flags a dN/dt anomaly in $NVDA and the capital flow prediction is right, the product is validated.

4. **Usage Analytics** — For UI usability (sidebar, broadsheet), watch where people click and how long they stay — but do NOT ask a committee what features to build.

## Distinction

- **Design validation (UI/UX):** Focus groups with 3+ personas are still the right tool. The `gazzetta-knowledge-base` Phase 11 rule stands: unilateral design changes without focus group validation cause regressions.
- **Product feature validation (what to build):** Focus groups are counterproductive. Use behavioral telemetry + syndicate feedback.
