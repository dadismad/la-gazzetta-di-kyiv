## Tracking Methodology — Components to Monitor

### A. Retirement Flows (401k / Defined Contribution)
- **Source:** ICI weekly fund flow data, BLS age-cohort employment
- **Signal:** 401k contribution growth rate; replacement ratio of contributors
- **Key metric:** Diminishment of 401k flows = early warning of flow regime shift

### B. Systematic Strategy Flows
- **Volatility Targeting:** Funds adjust equity exposure inversely to VIX → VIX drop = mechanical buying
- **CTAs (Trend-Following):** Breakouts force momentum buying; breakdowns force mechanical selling
- **Risk Parity:** Leverage bonds to match equity risk → rebalancing amplifies winners
- **Data:** CFTC Commitment of Traders, VIX futures term structure, brokerage flow reports

### C. Levered ETF Flows
- **Tracking twist:** $1 investor capital = $2-$3 underlying exposure
- **Important:** Track price IMPACT, not raw flow. Levered flows have ~√2x market impact
- **Data:** Daily creation/redemption from ETF issuers, Bloomberg

### D. Demographic Flow Pressure
- **Boomer retirement rate vs Millennial hiring rate** → replacement ratio
- **55-and-over hiring:** up 84% (per Riksbank study cited by Green)
- **Under-29 hiring:** down 25% → diminishing 401k contribution pipeline
- **Data:** BLS age-cohort employment, Fed Z.1 Flow of Funds

## Node-Based Capital Flow Decomposition (v22.22+)

Flat directional flows (inflow/outflow, $XB, sector) are the **first pass**. The second pass decomposes flows into **source→destination node pairs** — who sends capital and who receives it. This reveals adjacencies that flat flows hide: crypto flows adjacent to banking, government flows adjacent to defense contractors, retail 401k flows adjacent to institutional ETFs.

### Node Type Taxonomy (5 types)

| Type | Examples | Inbound Sources | Outbound Destinations |
|------|----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **GOVERNMENTAL** | Federal Reserve ($7.8T), US Treasury ($36.2T), Norway GPFG ($1.6T) | Tax receipts, bond issuance, sovereign wealth contributions | Bond markets, FX interventions, bailout facilities, defense procurement |
| **INSTITUTIONAL** | BlackRock ($11.5T), Bridgewater ($125B), pension funds | 401k contributions, institutional mandates, rebalancing flows | ETF creations, futures positions, private equity allocations |
| **CORPORATE** | Apple ($350B), MicroStrategy ($26B) | Revenue, debt issuance, equity offerings | Buybacks, M&A, dividends, supply chain payments |
| **RETAIL** | 401k/IRA ($38T), Retail Brokerage ($8.5T) | Payroll deductions, savings reallocation, stimulus | ETF purchases, meme stock flows, crypto exchange deposits |
| **CRYPTO** | Binance ($120B), Tether ($120B), DeFi protocols ($95B) | Exchange deposits, stablecoin minting, bridge inflows | DeFi lending, DEX swaps, stablecoin redemptions, bridge outflows |

## Integration Point

The node-based view is the **bottom-up** complement to the flows container's **top-down** aggregate view. Flows container says "$XB flowing into tech." Flow Nodes page says "Who sent it? Apple treasury → BlackRock AAPL ETF → retail 401k — 3 hops." This is the same data, decomposed.

### Flow Heat Score (v22.32+)
Every flow now carries a `heat_score` (0-100) — the percentile rank of `amount_b` among all 12 flows this cycle. 100 = largest flow. 8 = smallest.
```
heat_score = min(100, int((rank / n) * 100))
```
Displayed as a compact number next to each flow row. Color-coded: green ≥75 (extreme), gold 50-74 (elevated), grey <50 (normal). Answers the PM's question: "Is $30B a lot?" instantly.

### Trade Signal Derivation (v22.32+)
Per-flow `trade_signal` (BUY/SELL/WATCH) and `trade_emoji` (🟢🔴🟡). Derived from direction + divergence + confidence + pace:
```
Contrarian → BUY (if inflow) or SELL (if outflow)
Inflow + ≥90% confidence → BUY
Outflow + ≥85% confidence → SELL
Pace ≥2.5x → BUY (inflow) or SELL (outflow)
Otherwise → WATCH
```

### PDR — Passive Discovery Rate (v22.32+)
Estimated passive/active flow ratio per flow item. Real PDR needs EPFR fund-level data. Current estimation from amount + pace:
```
Large + slow (≥$25B, pace ≤2x) → PDR 1.5-2.1x, passive_dominant
  → "⚠ Passive-driven — likely mechanical rebalancing. Fade the signal."
Small + fast (≥$15B, pace ≥2.5x) → PDR 0.5-1.1x, active_conviction
  → "✓ Active conviction — genuine discretionary positioning. Follow."
Mid-range → PDR 0.8-1.4x, mixed
```
Displayed in expanded flow detail as: "PDR X.Xx · flow_type · recommendation"

### Mike Green Integration Framework (v22.32+)
Mike Green (@profplum99) × Degen Trader joint product design:
- PDR >1.5: passive dominates → fade the flow signal (it's mechanical, not conviction)
- PDR <0.7: active dominates → follow the flow signal (it's genuine positioning)
- Flow Heat Score 75+: extreme → pay attention (could be a regime change)
- Trade Signal 🟢🔴: one-tap actionable for degen timeframes
- Combined lens: macro analyst gets PDR decomposition, degen gets emoji signal. Same data, two views.

## Editorial Integration Pattern

### Format: Capital Flows Report Card
Interleaved into the story stack, full-width, using existing card template with distinct visual treatment:

- **Background:** `--gold-pale` (#F5F0E0) warm editorial highlight
- **Left border:** 4px solid `--gold` (#C8A44E)
- **Headline:** DM Serif Display 19px — editorial headline, not a data label
- **Data values:** Source Serif 4 17px semi-bold (600) — matches body rhythm
- **Labels/segments:** Inter 8-9px 700 uppercase — matches Bet&Benefit grammar
- **Flow direction:** `--green` (#27AE60) inflows / `--red` (#C0392B) outflows — same as Bet&Benefit
- **No gauges, no sparklines, no charts, no canvas, no rounded corners, no shadows**

### Frequency: Weekly Cadence
- **Tuesday 10:00 EET** — clean start-of-week trading data
- Published as dedicated card + `/flows` subpage for archive
- Editorial preamble: 2 sentences of context + data block + 1-sentence positioning

### Integration Rule: Contradiction-First
Only surface capital flow data when it CONTRADICTS the consensus narrative. If flows confirm consensus, skip. This preserves the contrarian DNA of Gazzetta and prevents data fatigue.

### Story Card Extension: CAPITAL FLOW Block (v19 Format)

Every story card MUST include a CAPITAL FLOW block between REALITY and THE PLAY. Format is 3 lines + confidence disclosure:

```
CAPITAL FLOW: $X.XB flowing [into/out of] [asset class] this week
Projected further flow: [+/-]$Y.YB (70% confidence)
Institutional positioning: [accumulating/hedging/distributing]
```

The confidence is displayed in the hero as **"Flow conviction: X% ↑"** (or ↓) with directional arrow derived from inflow/outflow ratio. This replaced the v22.6 "Directional alignment across N tracked flows: X%" after a retail-user focus group found the old label incomprehensible. The `aggregate_direction` field ("bullish"/"bearish") is generated by `generate_flows.py` at runtime — bullish when inflows ≥ outflows, bearish otherwise.

The institutional positioning tag must be one of: **accumulating**, **hedging**, or **distributing**. These map to Green's flow framework: accumulating = passive flows building, hedging = mixed signals, distributing = passive flows reversing.

**On the website**, the capital flow claim also appears as the FIRST LINE of each story card (bold, above the headline) in a compressed format:
```
$X.XB flowing into [asset] this week — projected +$Y.YB change at 70% confidence
```

Use this data even when flows CONFIRM consensus — the v19 user directive overrides the earlier contradiction-only rule. Every story gets a flow block, period.

### Triangulation Score Algorithm (v20.13 — capital-first weights)

```
Triangulation Score (0-100) = Flow Alignment + Bet Conviction + Event Strength + Alignment Bonus

FLOW ALIGNMENT (max 50 — capital is the prime mover):
  Amount tier:
    +20 if amount ≥ $5B
    +15 if amount $3B-$5B
    +10 if amount $1B-$3B
    +5  if amount < $1B
  Velocity tier (boosted for capital-first — pace matters more):
    +15 if pace ≥ 3.0x normal
    +12 if pace ≥ 2.5x normal
    +10 if pace ≥ 2.0x normal
    +7  if pace ≥ 1.5x normal
    +4  if pace < 1.5x normal
  Positioning:
    +10 if accumulating
    +8  if distributing
    +5  if hedging
  Stories with NO flow data receive 0 from this dimension → gated to low total scores

BET CONVICTION (max 30):
  +15 if anchor has BUY or SELL direction (not WATCH)
  +10 if conviction = HIGH
  +5  if conviction = MED
  +0  if conviction = LOW or WATCH

EVENT STRENGTH (max 20 — events without flow are noise):
  +10 if confidence = high
  +5  if contradiction present (they_say AND reality)
  +5  if extremum line present

ALIGNMENT BONUS (0 or +5):
  +5 if flow direction matches bet direction (inflow+BUY or outflow+SELL)
```

### Divergence Detection (The Real Edge)

A story scores lower when its signals conflict — but the **conflict itself is intelligence**:

- **Flow up + Bet DOWN** = money is flowing INTO an asset the anchor says to SELL. Either the flow is wrong (distribution incoming) or the anchor is wrong (position should flip). This is the highest-value editorial flag.
- **Flow down + Bet UP** = money is flowing OUT of an asset the anchor says to BUY. Same contradiction — find which signal is right.
- **Bet WATCH** = no directional conviction available. Score capped at 70 maximum.

When divergence is detected, editorial response: flag the story with ⚠️ DIVERGENCE and investigate which of the three signals is most reliable given recent data.

### Contradiction Scoring Algorithm (v20+)

The story card contradiction score measures actual narrative-vs-reality tension, NOT field existence. Replaced in June 2026 after the Macro Analyst focus group persona flagged the old algorithm as "decorative, not analytical."

**Old algorithm (REMOVED):** Field-existence check — `if (cf) score += 20; if (story.extremum) score += 15`. Every story scored 85-95. Zero discrimination.

**Current algorithm (4 dimensions, 0-100):**
1. Narrative-Reality Tension (0-30): contrast markers in reality text + substantive pushback check + proportional response
2. Flow-Narrative Divergence (0-25): narrative tone (pos/neg regex) vs flow direction + magnitude bonus
3. Extremum Quality (0-15): WINNER/LOSER pair + IDIOT/GENIUS pair + completeness bonus
4. Confidence Grounding (0-10): confidence level + data presence

Expected distribution: 30-95 (vs old flat 85-95). Scores 40-55 = developing contradictions, 60-75 = active tension, 80+ = acute divergence.

**Pitfall:** Regex word-matching on narrative tone (surge/boom/rally vs crash/fear/crisis). If they_say uses different wording, divergence = 0. For these stories, manually set narrative tone in story data.

Rule: If you can't identify the vehicle and the repricing trigger, it's gossip, not a story.

### Ukraine-Russia War — Three Highest-Value Capital Flow Angles
