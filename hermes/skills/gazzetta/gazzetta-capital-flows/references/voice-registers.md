## Voice Registers — The Gazzetta Spectrum

Gazzetta di Kyiv speaks in three registers, not one. The choice of register is a deliberate editorial decision per story — it signals who this story is for and what kind of money is in play.

### Register 1: THE CLAIM ("Young Money In Play")
**For:** $15K retail, crypto degens, the "simple idiot with FOMO"
**Signal:** This is YOUR money. Act now. The establishment is lying to you.
**Characteristics:**
- Direct address: "You," "Your move," "Here's the play"
- Short declarative sentences. One idea per line.
- Verbs of action: "Buy," "Sell," "Front-run," "Fade," "Ape in"
- Contempt for consensus: "They said transitory. You're down 18%."
- Ambition-forward: "This is how $15K becomes $50K in one quarter."
- Jargon translated immediately: "Gamma flip (when market makers turn against you)"

**Headline example:**
> "Bessent Called Inflation a 'Blip.' Your Rent Is Up 22%. Here's How to Make It Pay."

### Register 2: THE BRIEF ("Semi-Professional Conviction")
**For:** Active traders with $50K-500K, the CFA-failed retail trader who knows the basics
**Signal:** You understand the game. Here's the edge the pros don't want you to see.
**Characteristics:**
- Ticker-first, number-dense, thesis-driven
- Assumes knowledge of options, spreads, yield curves, vol surfaces
- Jargon used without apology: "IV skew," "gamma wall," "steepener," "CDS basis"
- Contrarian framing: "Consensus says X. The flow data says Y. You know which side pays."
- Actionable precision: "Sell the $1,150/$1,170 call spread, collect $8.40, stop at $4.20."

**Headline example:**
> "NVDA Gamma Wall at $1,140 — Every Dollar Below Triggers $2.8B in Dealer Selling. Fade the Bounce."

### Register 3: THE DISPATCH ("Victory Claim")
**For:** Institutional-adjacent, the ambitious semi-pro who wants to SOUND like a pro
**Signal:** We see the board. We know the players. We claim the winnings.
**Characteristics:**
- Dense, confident, almost arrogant
- Named actors, specific instruments, precise levels
- Multi-asset framing: "The trade isn't NVDA. It's NVDA vol vs SOXX vol, front-month."
- Historical context: "Last time CDS widened this fast was February 2022. The play paid 14:1."
- Macro sweep: cross-asset, cross-geography, regime-aware
- Bloomberg-adjacent: precision and authority without the sterility

**Headline example:**
> "Ukraine CDS +120bps in 72 Hours — The Front-Month 27/30 Put Spread Just Repriced to 14:1. Last Time This Happened: February 2022."

### Register Selection Rules

| Story Type | Default Register | When to Shift |
|------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Corruption scandal | THE DISPATCH | Shift to THE BRIEF if the trade is simple |
| Crypto/on-chain | THE CLAIM | Shift to THE BRIEF for options flow |
| Macro/rates | THE BRIEF | Shift to THE DISPATCH for cross-asset |
| Energy/commodities | THE DISPATCH | Shift to THE CLAIM for retail entry points |
| Defense/war | THE DISPATCH | Shift to THE BRIEF for specific stock plays |
| THE PLAY OF THE DAY | THE CLAIM | Always THE CLAIM — one trade, one button |

### The Rule: Swagger Earned, Never Borrowed

Every claim of victory must be backed by a specific number. Every display of confidence must cite a source. This is not bravado — it's the calm of someone who did the work and knows what happens next.

**✓ Allowed:** "Ukraine CDS widened 120bps in 72 hours. The 14:1 payout from Feb '22 is back on the table."
**✗ Forbidden:** "Massive opportunities ahead as smart money positions for the next leg."

### The Ambition Signal

Words that cry "victory and money in play":
- **Verbs:** claim, capture, seize, front-run, position for, rotate into, extract
- **Nouns:** edge, asymmetry, conviction, the play, the board, the claim
- **Modifiers:** asymmetric, structural, non-consensus, flow-confirmed, institutionally-ignored
- **Numbers:** Always. Specific. Verifiable. "14:1" not "significant upside."

Words that kill ambition (banned):
- "Opportunity," "potential upside," "could be interesting," "worth watching"
- "We believe," "in our view," "it appears that" — hedge language for people afraid to be wrong
- "Significant," "substantial," "meaningful" — placeholder adjectives for people who didn't do the math
