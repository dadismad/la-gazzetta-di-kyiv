## Core Theses

### 1. Passive Flow Thesis
Passive investing (>50% of US equity AUM) has broken price discovery. Active managers would sell overvalued / buy undervalued; passive flows are mechanical (buy everything, sell nothing). This creates an "absorption ratio" that suppresses volatility — until it doesn't. Green calls passive flows "a car with no steering wheel."

### 2. Demographic Inversion
Aging boomers sell assets to fund consumption; working-age population (buyers) shrinking. This structural headwind is masked by central bank liquidity and passive inflow tailwinds. Green calls this the "Great Inversion" — the biggest secular macro force nobody prices.

### 3. Volatility Illusion
Passive flows create a self-reinforcing cycle: low volatility → more passive inflows → further volatility suppression → sudden unwind. Persistent VIX contango is the tell.

### 4. Fed Put vs. Structural Drain
The Fed's liquidity injections mask demographic sell pressure. But QT + TGA rebuild + RRP drain = net liquidity declining. Green watches the "triple drain": Fed balance sheet, TGA, RRP.
