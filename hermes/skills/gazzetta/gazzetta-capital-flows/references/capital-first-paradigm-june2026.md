# Capital-First Paradigm — Focus Group Validation (June 2026)

## Paradigm Shift
**From:** Stories drive capital flows (narrative-first)  
**To:** Capital moves markets. Capital creates stories. Capital forces action. (capital-first)

## Multi-Lens Validation (3 Personas)

### Capital Flow Analyst (Hedge Fund) — 6.5/10
- "Architecture succeeds at being capital-first. Content is 60% capital-derived, 40% traditional curation."
- Key gaps: no temporal delta (week-over-week trend), no price correlation overlay, no flow breakdown by investor type, empty capital.html methodology page
- 4 of 6 flow→story→bet chains are coherent; 2 break when bet is WATCH or missing

### Systems Architect (Data Pipeline) — 5/10
- "Containers are correctly ordered but Stories are human editorial with flow labels stapled on."
- Critical gap: stories exist without flow data (4 of 10 have "FLOW: none")
- Recommendation: flow-triggered narrative generation — stories shouldn't exist until flow data appears
- Pipeline should be monotonic: Flow Anomaly Detected → Sector Identified → Template Generates → Human adds THEY SAY/REALITY overlay

### Financial Product Manager (Newsletters) — 8/10 commercial viability
- "Most compelling newsletter format shift I've seen. $500/mo institutional product repackaged for retail."
- Killer feature needed: real-time flow alerts with push-to-execute thresholds
- Biggest risk: no published signal track record — must prove flow+bet+event alignment outperforms 55-60%

## Changes Applied (v20.12–v20.13)

| Element | Before | After |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Container order | ANCHOR→SIGNAL→STORIES→FLOWS | FLOWS→ANCHOR→SIGNAL→STORIES |
| Signal scoring | Event 35 / Flow 35 / Bet 30 | Flow 50 / Bet 30 / Event 20 |
| Velocity tiers | 2 tiers (<1.5, ≥1.5, ≥2.5) | 5 tiers (<1.5, ≥1.5, ≥2.0, ≥2.5, ≥3.0) |
| Stories name | "What's moving markets right now" | "What the capital is saying" |
| Masthead | "The stories that move markets" | "Capital moves markets. We track where it's going." |
| Signal subtitle | Event × Flow × Bet | Flow × Bet × Event |
| Flows description | Generic tracking text | "Capital moves markets. Capital creates stories. Capital forces action." |
