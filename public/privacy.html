Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

1. What We Collect

La Gazzetta di Kyiv does not require user accounts, does not use cookies for tracking, and does not collect personal information through the website. We do not serve third-party advertisements or analytics trackers.

2. Server Logs

Our hosting provider (Google Cloud Storage) may collect standard server logs including IP addresses, request timestamps, and user agents. These are used solely for operational monitoring and are not shared with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We link to Telegram and Reddit for community engagement. These platforms have their own privacy policies. We use Google Fonts for typography, which may collect usage data per Google's privacy policy.

4. Data Sources

All financial data displayed is aggregated from public sources (EPFR Global, Morningstar Direct, CoinGecko) and internal models. No personal financial data is collected or stored.
