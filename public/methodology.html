Methodology & Verification

45|

Every number on La Gazzetta di Kyiv is computed, not guessed. This page documents the mathematical framework, data pipelines, and the 183-assertion test suite that gates every deploy.

46| 47|

1. Asymmetry Score — Price-Narrative Delta

48|

Formula

49|
50| Score = |Sentiment − PriceDelta| × 50
51|
52| Where:
53|   Sentiment ∈ [−1, 1] = narrative direction × confidence / 100
54|   PriceDelta ∈ [−1, 1] = tanh(24h_price_change_pct / 5)
55|   Tiers: ≥80 MAX · ≥65 HIGH · ≥40 MODERATE · <40 LOW
56|
57|

The Asymmetry Score measures the gap between what the narrative says and what the price is doing. A score of 76 means the narrative is strongly contradicted by price action — this is where alpha lives.

58| 59|

2. Conviction Probability

60|

Formula

61|
62| Probability = contradiction_base + source_bonus + freshness_bonus + confidence_bonus
63|
64| contradiction_base = 50 + min((contradiction_score − 45) × 0.8, 35)
65| source_bonus = min((sources − 1) × 5, 15)   — +5% per corroborating source, max +15%
66| freshness_bonus = 10 for BREAKING, 5 for 1-24h horizon, 0 otherwise
67| confidence_bonus = 10 for HIGH, 5 for MEDIUM, 0 for LOW
68|
69| Clamped to [50%, 95%]. ALPHA tier ≥ 85%. 70|
71| 72|

3. Story-Level Scaling (SLS)

73|

Formula

74|
75| Story Amount = Category Total × tier_fraction × pillar_bonus × (1 + jitter_pct/100)
76|
77| tier_fractions: BREAKING=0.35 · DEVELOPING=0.20 · ACTIVE=0.12 · SETTLING=0.05
78| pillar_bonus: geoeconomic/sovereign=1.15, else 1.0
79| jitter: deterministic ±5% from headline hash 80|
81|

Each story receives a unique capital flow amount — no two stories on the homepage show the same number. The total category flow ($88B for commodities) distributes across individual stories based on tier priority and pillar significance.

82| 83|

4. ATR-Based Stop Calculation

84|
85| Stop = Entry ± (Entry × ATR% × Stop_Multiplier)
86|
87| ATR%: asset-specific 14-day Average True Range as % of price
88| Stop Mult: BUY=Entry×(1−ATR%×mult) · SELL=Entry×(1+ATR%×mult)
89|
90| Examples: SPX ATR=1.2%×2.0→Stop=−2.4% · NVDA ATR=3.5%×2.0→Stop=−7.0% 91|
