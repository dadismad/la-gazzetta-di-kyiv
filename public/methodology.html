Methodology & Verification

Every number on La Gazzetta di Kyiv is computed, not guessed. This page documents the mathematical framework, data pipelines, and the 183-assertion test suite that gates every deploy.

1. Asymmetry Score — Price-Narrative Delta

Formula

Score = |Sentiment − PriceDelta| × 50

Where:
  Sentiment ∈ [−1, 1] = narrative direction × confidence / 100
  PriceDelta ∈ [−1, 1] = tanh(24h_price_change_pct / 5)
  Tiers: ≥80 MAX · ≥65 HIGH · ≥40 MODERATE · <40 LOW

The Asymmetry Score measures the gap between what the narrative says and what the price is doing. A score of 76 means the narrative is strongly contradicted by price action — this is where alpha lives.

2. Conviction Probability

Formula

Probability = contradiction_base + source_bonus + freshness_bonus + confidence_bonus

contradiction_base = 50 + min((contradiction_score − 45) × 0.8, 35)
source_bonus = min((sources − 1) × 5, 15)   — +5% per corroborating source, max +15%
freshness_bonus = 10 for BREAKING, 5 for 1-24h horizon, 0 otherwise
confidence_bonus = 10 for HIGH, 5 for MEDIUM, 0 for LOW

Clamped to [50%, 95%]. ALPHA tier ≥ 85%.

3. Story-Level Scaling (SLS)

Formula

Story Amount = Category Total × tier_fraction × pillar_bonus × (1 + jitter_pct/100)

tier_fractions: BREAKING=0.35 · DEVELOPING=0.20 · ACTIVE=0.12 · SETTLING=0.05
pillar_bonus: geoeconomic/sovereign=1.15, else 1.0
jitter: deterministic ±5% from headline hash

Each story receives a unique capital flow amount — no two stories on the homepage show the same number. The total category flow ($88B for commodities) distributes across individual stories based on tier priority and pillar significance.

4. ATR-Based Stop Calculation

Stop = Entry ± (Entry × ATR% × Stop_Multiplier)

ATR%: asset-specific 14-day Average True Range as % of price
Stop Mult: BUY=Entry×(1−ATR%×mult) · SELL=Entry×(1+ATR%×mult)

Examples: SPX ATR=1.2%×2.0→Stop=−2.4% · NVDA ATR=3.5%×2.0→Stop=−7.0%

5. Pipeline Integrity — 183 Assertions

✓ 183 PASSING Every deploy runs through test_platform.py which verifies:

  • Anti-lying: No $0.0, undefined, or $5.0B default fallbacks in HTML
  • Data integrity: stories.json ↔ flows.json bidirectional consistency
  • Schema validation: All required fields present on every story
  • Translation sync: EN↔RU story count parity (±5 tolerance)
  • Flow normalization: Direction strings normalized, no orphan flows
  • Cache integrity: Cache-Control: max-age=0 on all .json and .html
  • Container non-emptiness: All 7 product containers must render content

6. Data Sources

  • yfinance: Live price data (BTC-USD, SPY, WTI, GOLD, QQQ, TLT, UUP, ITA)
  • Telegram Intel: Curated geopolitical and macro intelligence feeds
  • CFTC COT: Commitment of Traders reports (weekly)
  • ICI Fund Flows: Mutual fund and ETF flow data (weekly)
  • EPFR Global: Institutional capital flow tracking (delayed)
  • OSINT: Reuters, AP, Bloomberg wire feeds (filtered for signal)

7. Cloud Infrastructure

Gazzetta di Kyiv runs 24/7 on a Google Cloud e2-micro VM (Always Free tier). Four systemd timers orchestrate the pipeline:

  • gazzetta-intel.timer — Source monitoring (30m)
  • gazzetta-pipeline.timer — DB→JSON compilation + asymmetry computation (60m)
  • gazzetta-marketdata.timer — yfinance price fetch (6h)
  • gazzetta-shipit.timer — GCS deploy (60m)

Static assets served from Google Cloud Storage with Cloud CDN. Monthly infrastructure cost: $0.00.
