The Belt and Road Initiative, despite its well-documented debt sustainability problems, has physically connected 140+ countries to Chinese infrastructure standards, Chinese industrial equipment, and Chinese development financing. This is not charity — it is the physical layer of a new capital architecture. When a BRI-funded port in East Africa runs on Huawei 5G infrastructure, financed by China Exim Bank, with commodities priced in RMB futures, the dollar is irrelevant to that transaction chain. The BRICS expansion (Iran, Egypt, Ethiopia, UAE, Saudi Arabia) and the New Development Bank represent the institutional layer. The media covers BRICS as a geopolitical talking shop. The capital flows show bilateral trade settlement in RMB growing at 35% annually. The domestic challenges — property sector overhang, demographic decline, regulatory opacity — are real and suppress headline valuations (FXI trades at a structural discount). But the capital architecture being built externally operates on a different timeline and a different risk calculus.
