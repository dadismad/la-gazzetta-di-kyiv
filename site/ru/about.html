About

La Gazzetta di Kyiv is a capital flow intelligence publication. We track where institutional money is moving — before prices reflect it.

Our editorial method is contradiction-first: every story exposes the gap between what the market believes (They Say) and what the data shows (Reality). This gap is where alpha lives.

Editorial Philosophy

5-Layer Intelligence Pipeline:

  1. Stories — Narrative decoded through contradiction analysis
  2. Capital Flows — Institutional money movement with directional conviction
  3. Trade Ideas — Actionable positions with entry, target, and stop levels
  4. Signal Triangulation — Cross-referencing stories × flows × positions
  5. Track Record — Verifiable bet performance, no cherry-picking

Data & Methodology

Our capital flow data is sourced from EPFR Global, Morningstar Direct, and internal aggregation. Velocity is measured against 4-week rolling averages. Confidence scores use a transparent 4-factor model (flow magnitude + pace + positioning + contradiction). Full methodology: capital.html.

Trade stops are derived from ATR (Average True Range) with 2-3× multipliers based on volatility regime. All predictions are timestamped. Track Record is verifiable — settled against realized prices.

Limitations

This is an intelligence product, not investment advice. Flow data has a 3-5 day reporting lag. Confidence models have not been backtested. Past performance does not guarantee future results. All trade ideas carry risk of loss. Do your own research.
