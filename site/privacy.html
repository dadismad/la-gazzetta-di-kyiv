Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. La Gazzetta di Kyiv is a static intelligence publication. We do not use cookies, tracking scripts, analytics, user accounts, or any form of personal data collection.

The only data that touches our infrastructure is:

  • Standard server logs — Google Cloud Storage automatically logs HTTP requests (IP address, timestamp, URL, user agent). These logs are not analyzed, mined, or shared.
  • Language preference — If you switch between English and Russian, your choice is stored in your browser's localStorage. This never leaves your device.

2. How We Use Information

We don't. There is nothing to use. We have no ad network, no analytics platform, no newsletter signup collecting emails, and no comment system.

3. Third-Party Services

Our site is hosted on Google Cloud Storage with Google Cloud CDN. Google's standard infrastructure logging applies. We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

When you share our content via the share buttons (X, Facebook, Telegram, Reddit), you are subject to those platforms' respective privacy policies.

4. Data Security

All traffic to lagazzettadikyiv.com is encrypted via HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). Our SSL certificate is issued by Google Trust Services.

5. Your Rights

Since we collect no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, delete, or port. If you believe otherwise, contact us immediately.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we ever add data collection (unlikely — it contradicts our ethos), we will update this policy with at least 30 days' notice.
